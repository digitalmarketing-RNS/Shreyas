# 60 places to submit

You asked for 60, so here are 60, named and numbered. Composed deliberately:
the directory and profile portion stays inside safe bounds, and the rest comes
from places that pass real link equity.

Mark what you have already done. The tracker only helps if it reflects reality.

---

## What 60 profile links will and will not do

Straight, because you are about to spend real hours on this.

An independent 2026 review of profile-creation tactics puts it this way: the
value is brand presence, citations and referral traffic, **not backlinks**, and
almost all profile links are nofollow. Mass profile creation is a tactic Google
names in its link spam documentation. The same source recommends 30 to 50 quality
platforms rather than mass submission.

So:

- **Groups A to E (45 entries)** build entity consistency, brand SERP control
  and AI-search citability. Mostly nofollow. Worth doing once, properly
- **Groups F to H (15 entries)** are where followed links and referral traffic
  actually live, because they require you to produce or participate rather than
  register

If you complete all 60 and nothing else, rankings will not move much. The three
things that would move them are still: your clients linking to you, guest posts
on IIDM and similar, and original research. None of those are on this list
because they are in `06-outreach.md` and they are worth more than all 60 combined.

That is the honest framing. Now the list.

## How to work through it

**Pace it.** Six to eight weeks, not one weekend. A new domain gaining 60
referring domains in days is the velocity pattern that triggers review.

**Order:**

1. Group B first. Check every partner eligibility. If Google Partners is open to
   you, it outranks everything else here
2. Numbers 20 to 22, if not already done
3. Group A, top five
4. Group H, because it takes calendar time to arrange
5. Everything else, filling in gradually
6. Group F only if you commit to publishing

**Track it.** Add a row per target in `02-tracker.csv` with the date and the live
URL. Without that you will resubmit, lose track of what is live, and never know
which of the 60 produced anything.

**The check at week eight:** open Search Console, look at Links, and count how
many of the 60 actually appear. My expectation is roughly half will never be
indexed as links to you. That number is the useful output of this exercise, and
it will tell you where to spend the next quarter better than any list will.
