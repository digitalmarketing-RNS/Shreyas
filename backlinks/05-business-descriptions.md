## Why it reads this way

Run through the `humanizer` skill. No em dashes, no "comprehensive solutions",
no "passionate about driving results", no rule-of-three lists. Every directory in
your category is full of that copy, which is exactly why none of it registers.

The load-bearing paragraph is the two-bad-options one. It names a problem your
buyer has already had, which is more persuasive than any adjective, and it is
the only part a competitor cannot copy without describing their own weakness.

One thing to check yourself: **is "founder stays on the account" actually why
clients pick you?** I inferred it from your public copy. If the real reason is
something else, faster reporting, a specific industry you know cold, pricing,
then that paragraph should say that instead. It is the sentence doing the most
work, so it is the one worth getting right.
