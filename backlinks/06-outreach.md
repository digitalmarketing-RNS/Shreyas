# Move 2 — better links, and the emails to get them

---

## Read this first: you are already inside a reciprocal link pattern

Searching your name turned up two things at once.

**Good:** you are already named in Bangalore freelancer roundups. Search results
quote "Shreyas V Patil is recognized as one of the leading digital marketing
freelancers in Bangalore, with 10 years of hands-on experience."

**Risky:** almost every one of those roundups is **another freelancer's own
website**. shakirdm360.in, priyankatanwar.in, digitalmarketingwithpriya.com,
mahakdigital.com, preetispace.com, arsiyahub.com, krishnaseo.com,
jijojosephseo.in, anandkjha.com. These are competitors publishing "top 10" lists
that feature each other. And you publish one too:
`eeatminds.in/post/digital-marketing-freelancers-in-india`.

That is a link circle. The backlink-quality reference lists "reciprocal link
patterns across 10+ domains" as a likely-spam pattern requiring manual review,
and the skill's own pre-delivery checklist requires flagging it when site A links
to site B and B links back.

**What to do:**

- **Do** reclaim mentions that already exist. That is not reciprocal, it is
  correcting an omission.
- **Do not** trade listings. The moment an email says "I'll add you if you add
  me", you are building the pattern deliberately.
- **Do** get your next 10 links from outside this circle. A profile where every
  referring domain is a competing Bangalore freelancer is a thin, correlated
  profile no matter how many entries it has.

Your own roundup post is fine as a page. Just do not use it as trade currency.

## Better link targets, ranked

Ordered by value per hour, not by ease.

### 1. Google Partners and Meta Business Partners

You state Google and Meta certification. If that is a real partner status rather
than individual exam certificates, both run public partner directories. High
authority, genuinely earned, tied to credentials you already hold, and outside
the freelancer link circle entirely. **Check this first.** It may be the single
best link available to you and it costs nothing.

If it is individual certification rather than partner status, note the difference
honestly in your copy. Google Partner status has revenue and account thresholds.

### 2. Unlinked mention reclamation

You are already named on the roundups above. Check each for whether the mention
carries a live link to eeatminds.in. Where it does not, ask. Highest hit rate in
all of link building because you are not asking for a favour, you are asking
someone to finish a sentence they already wrote.

Verify with: `site:shakirdm360.in "Shreyas"` and so on for each domain.

### 3. Client links

Your clients' own sites. "Marketing by EEAT Minds" in a footer, or a mention in
their about page. Most freelancers never ask. These are relevant, contextual,
and from real businesses in varied industries, which is exactly the diversity
your profile currently lacks. Ask the three clients who like you most.

### 4. Source of Sources journalist requests

Free, up to three digests a day. You answer a reporter's question properly and
get credited. Requires speed: popular requests draw hundreds of replies in the
first hour. This is the guide's Move 2 core and it is genuinely the cleanest
editorial link available to a small operator.

### 5. Guest posts on educational marketing publications

### 6. Podcasts

Marketing and Indian startup podcasts. Show notes almost always carry a link,
the audience matches your buyer, and there is no editorial gatekeeping beyond
being interesting for forty minutes. Underused because it feels like more work
than it is.

### 7. Bangalore local and community

Chamber of commerce, coworking spaces you use, startup community pages, local
business associations. Geographically relevant, which matters for your local
pack, and outside the competitor circle.

## About recipient email addresses

**I cannot verify a single email address from this session.** Every one of these
sites is blocked by the network policy here, so I cannot open a contact page to
read the real address.

I will not guess one. A fabricated address bounces, and repeated bounces damage
your sending reputation on a domain you just set up. That is a worse outcome than
sending nothing.

**Find the real address this way, in order:**

1. The site's own contact or about page. Most of these freelancer sites publish
   an address openly because they want client enquiries
2. Their LinkedIn, often listed in the contact panel
3. The WHOIS record for smaller sites
4. Hunter.io free tier, 25 searches a month, and it shows a confidence score
5. For publications, the byline author's name plus the site's contact form

**Never send to a guessed address.** If you cannot find one, use the contact
form. Lower response rate, zero bounce risk.

## The emails

Send from `shreyas@eeatminds.in`, not Gmail. One at a time. Never in a batch,
never with a mail-merge tool, never with tracking pixels on a first touch.

### Email 1 — unlinked mention reclamation

Highest hit rate. Use where a roundup names you but does not link.

**Subject:** `quick thing about your Bangalore freelancers post`

```
Hi [name],

Thanks for including me in your Bangalore digital marketing freelancers list.
Genuinely appreciated.

One small thing. My name is in there but it is not linked, so anyone reading it
has to go and search for me. If you are able to point it at
https://www.eeatminds.in/ that would help a lot.

Either way, thanks for the mention.

Shreyas V Patil
EEAT Minds, Bangalore
```

Why it works: you are not asking for a new favour, you are asking someone to
complete something they already chose to do. No pitch, no swap offer, under 70
words.

## Sending rules

- **Five emails a day, maximum.** You are on a new domain with no sending
  history. Volume from a cold domain lands in spam and the reputation damage
  outlasts the campaign
- **One follow-up only**, seven days later, three lines, then stop. A second
  follow-up converts almost nobody and annoys the rest
- **No tracking pixels on first contact.** Some filters flag them, and it reads
  badly if noticed
- **Personalise the first line every time.** Not the name merge, the first line.
  If you cannot write a specific opening sentence about their site, you have not
  earned the send
- **Log every one** in `02-tracker.csv` so you never email the same person twice

Expect roughly 1 in 10 to reply on cold pitches. Mention reclamation and client
requests will run far higher, which is why they are first on the list.
