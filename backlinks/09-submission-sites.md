# Expanded submission list

Adds to the Foundation set in `01-foundation-targets.md`. Everything here is
named, categorised, and rated for fit with a Bangalore digital marketing
services business.

---

## The hard limit, before the list

The toxic-pattern reference flags **mass directory submissions at 50+ directory
links** as a high-risk indicator, auto-flagged. Generic directories with no
editorial standard sit at #25, monitor.

So the answer to "give me a big list" is deliberately not a big list.
**Target 25 to 30 submissions total, all chosen for relevance.** More than that
and the profile starts describing you as a directory-blaster rather than a
Bangalore marketing practice.

You will find pages titled "125+ Free Business Listing Sites" and "100 High DA
Instant Approval Directories." Ignore them. Two tells that a directory is not
worth your time:

- **"Instant approval"** as a selling point. It means no editorial review, which
  means everyone is in, which means the link means nothing
- **The list itself is the product.** Sites publishing 125-item directory lists
  are farming the "business listing sites" keyword, not vetting anything

## Group 1: agency and services directories

Where your buyers actually look. Highest value per submission for you.

| Target | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Clutch** | Excellent | The default. Free profile, review-gated ranking. Start review outreach now |
| **GoodFirms** | Excellent | Strongest India coverage. 10,000+ Indian agencies verified |
| **DesignRush** | Strong | Curates "best of" roundups and agency spotlights, so a listing can lead to editorial inclusion |
| **UpCity** | Strong | Established, free basic profile |
| **The Manifest** | Strong | Clutch sister property, reuses your Clutch data. Near-zero extra effort once Clutch is done |
| **FindBestFirms** | Moderate | Free basic profile, commonly listed alongside the above |
| **SelectedFirms** | Moderate | Smaller, low effort |
| **Agency Spotter** | Moderate | Established but US-weighted |
| **Sortlist** | Weak | Directory appearance paywalled from EUR99/mo. Free profile only. See tracker |

**Do the first five.** The rest are optional once those are complete and reviewed.

## Group 2: partner and certification directories

**The highest-authority links available to you, and they cannot be bought.**
Check eligibility for each.

| Target | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Google Partners** | Partner status, which has spend and certification thresholds. Different from holding individual exam certificates. Verify which you actually have |
| **Meta Business Partners** | Similar tiering |
| **Semrush Agency Partners** | Requires a paid Semrush subscription. If you already pay for it, the listing is free value you are leaving on the table |
| **HubSpot Solutions Directory** | Requires partner programme membership |

If you hold any of these, do them before anything in Group 1. Earned,
authoritative, and outside every pattern flagged elsewhere in this workspace.

## Group 3: India local and business directories

Priority order. Note the fit column, because several sites that appear on every
"Indian directory" list are wrong for a services business.

| Target | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | Essential | Already priority 1. Local intent wins the map pack |
| **Bing Places** | Essential | Feeds ChatGPT and Copilot. Your AI-search lever |
| **Apple Business Connect** | Strong | Free, few competitors bother, feeds Apple Maps and Siri |
| **JustDial** | Strong | High traffic in India. Expect sales calls |
| **Sulekha** | Moderate | 17,700+ competing listings, so visibility is low, but a cheap citation |
| **Yellow Pages India** | Moderate | Old and trusted, weaker for digital services |
| **Hotfrog India** | Moderate | Low effort |
| **Asklaila** | Weak | Bangalore-origin, local relevance |
| **Grotal** | Weak | Low value, only if completing the set |
| **IndiaMART** | Weak fit | Built for products and B2B trade, not services |
| **TradeIndia** | Poor fit | Manufacturers, exporters, wholesalers. Not you |
| **ExportersIndia** | Skip | Export trade. Irrelevant |
| **Urban Company** | Skip | Home services: electricians, plumbers, beauticians. Not a marketing channel for you |

**Do the top four.** Add Sulekha, Yellow Pages and Hotfrog if you want the local
citation set complete. Skip the bottom four regardless of their DA. A high-DA
link from an irrelevant directory is the "links from unrelated niches" pattern
at #20.

## Group 4: freelance marketplaces

You position as a freelancer, so these fit the brand. Treat them as **lead
sources first, links second** since most profile links are nofollow.

| Target | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Upwork** | Largest. Real lead flow for Bangalore SEO |
| **Fiverr** | Volume-driven, lower price expectations |
| **PeoplePerHour** | UK-weighted, decent rates |
| **Guru** | Lower competition |
| **Freelancer.com** | High volume, low margin |
| **Toptal** | Screened network, hard to enter, strong if accepted |

Pick two, not six. A thin profile on six platforms is worse than an active one
on two.

## Group 5: professional and entity

| Target | Notes |
|---|---|
| **LinkedIn company page** | Exists. Optimise |
| **LinkedIn personal** | For a founder-led practice this matters more than most directories |
| **Crunchbase** | Entity and knowledge-graph value |
| **Wellfound** (ex AngelList) | Startup ecosystem visibility |
| **F6S** | Startup profile, low effort |
| **Medium** | Only if you will publish. A dormant profile is worthless |
| **Wikidata** | Only with genuine notability |

## Group 6: Bangalore local and community

Underused, genuinely relevant, and outside the competitor circle entirely.

- Bangalore Chamber of Industry and Commerce
- FKCCI, Karnataka's state chamber
- Your coworking space's member directory, if you use one
- Local startup community and meetup pages
- Alumni directory for your college or university
- BNI or similar local business networking chapters

These take real-world effort, which is exactly why almost none of your
competitors have them, and why they are worth more per link than the entire
bottom half of Group 3.

## Recommended total

| Group | Do |
|---|---|
| Agency directories | 5 |
| Partner directories | However many you qualify for |
| India local | 4 to 7 |
| Freelance marketplaces | 2 |
| Professional and entity | 4 to 5 |
| Bangalore local | 3 to 5 |

**Roughly 25 to 30.** Spread across six to eight weeks, not one. Comfortably
under the 50-link auto-flag, and every entry defensible as relevant to a
Bangalore marketing practice.

## The order that matters

Google Business Profile and Bing Places first, this week. Free, no review
gating, no paywall, and together they carry more weight for "digital marketing
freelancer in Bangalore" than every remaining directory on this page combined.

Then check your partner eligibility, because if Google Partners is available to
you it outranks everything in Group 1.

Then Clutch and GoodFirms, with review outreach already running.

Everything after that is filling in a long tail with diminishing returns.
