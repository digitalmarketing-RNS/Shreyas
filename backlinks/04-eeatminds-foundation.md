## Five things worth fixing before you submit anything

**1. The Gmail address.** `eeatminds.in@gmail.com` is the weakest signal on the
whole profile. You sell digital marketing; a free mailbox undercuts that before
anyone reads a word. Clutch and GoodFirms both weight a domain-matched email
during verification, and some reject free-provider addresses. Set up
`shreyas@eeatminds.in` first. It costs an hour and it raises the ceiling on
every listing below.

**2. Confirm the numbers.** See above. Do this before submission, not after.

**3. The openpr.com press release.** Press-release syndication links are
usually no-follow and carry little weight. One is harmless. A pattern of them
sits on the toxic-pattern list in the backlink-quality reference (#23, "links
from press release syndication networks"), so if anyone is selling you a PR
distribution package, that is the reason to say no.

**4. Swap-test your city pages.** You have Bangalore and Chennai pages. Take the
Chennai page, replace "Chennai" with "Bangalore", and read it. If it still makes
sense, Google reads it as a doorway page. You are under the 30-page warning gate
so there is no crisis, but an SEO business getting demoted for doorway pages is
a bad story. Add something real per city: local client work, area-specific
pricing, named neighbourhoods.

**5. Start review outreach today.** Clutch and GoodFirms rank on verified client
reviews, and those take weeks because a client has to complete an interview or
form. The profile is free and fast; the reviews are the long pole. Pick your
three happiest clients and ask this week, in parallel with everything else.

## Sequencing

Do not submit all sixteen this week. A brand-new domain acquiring links in a
burst is the exact velocity pattern the skill flags as manipulation.

- **Week 1** — branded email, then Tier 1 (items 1 to 5). Start review outreach.
- **Weeks 2 to 3** — Tier 2 (6 to 9), as reviews come in
- **Weeks 4 to 6** — Tier 3 (10 to 13), two or three per week
- **Week 6+** — Tier 4, and only then Move 2

Target is 30 to 50 referring domains by day 90, arriving steadily.
