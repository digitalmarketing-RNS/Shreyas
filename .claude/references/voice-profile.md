## 3. Hard rules (always / never)

- Always: one real, checkable number per post; name the brand or tool; first person; end on a specific question
- Always: real people on camera in brand content; AI for the craft around them
- Never: claim a result that isn't confirmed; self-awarded superlatives ("best", "No. 1"); links in the post body; comment-gate CTAs ("comment X to get Y")
