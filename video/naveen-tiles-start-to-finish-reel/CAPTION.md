## Wording notes

- The video says "state-of-the-art" and "precision engineered". It does not say "fully automated" or "by machine". People appear in the footage (operators, the loading crew at dispatch), so an automation claim would be easy to challenge.
- "State-of-the-art" uses the website's own wording ("state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities", Manufacturers page).
- The spec figures (252 m kilns, 6,500 t presses, 64-head lines, 860 lakh sq ft/yr) are company-wide figures from naveentile.com. They are worded as "up to", not as figures for this one plant.
