## Posting checklist

- Upload the video as a Reel and pick `NaveenTiles_FactoryReel_cover.jpg` as the cover (Edit cover → Add from camera roll).
- If you use a trending sound, upload the no-music version and turn "Original audio" down to about 30% so the machine sounds stay underneath.
- Tag the brand account and location on the post. If you have a website or showroom, pin a comment with it.
- The last card says "Follow for more factory stories". To show a handle or phone number instead, change `cta` in `src/Root.tsx` and re-render.
