## Posting checklist

- Upload the video as a Reel and pick `NaveenTiles_FactoryReel_cover.jpg` as the cover (Edit cover → Add from camera roll).
- Instagram allows 5 hashtags. Keep the 5 above and don't add more.
- Location tag: the plant at Sira, Tumakuru.
- URLs in captions aren't clickable, so naveentile.com should be the link in bio.
- If you use a trending sound, upload the no-music version and turn "Original audio" down to about 30% so the factory sounds stay underneath.
- Post this one first and the 78 s "start to finish" reel 3–4 days later.
- The last card says "Follow for more factory stories". To show a handle or phone number instead, change `cta` in `src/Root.tsx` and re-render.
