## Before you post

1. **Upload one video, not two.** A LinkedIn post can only hold one video
   (LinkedIn Help, "Share a video on LinkedIn FAQ"). Both reels have been joined
   into one file: `RNS-Group-AI-Reels-LinkedIn-25MB.mp4`, 51.5 s, vertical
   1080x1920, robots first and then the school. It was re-encoded to about
   25 MB so it could be sent through chat. LinkedIn compresses every upload
   below that anyway. It's not in the repo because of its size. It was sent
   to you in the chat.
2. **Tag the brands by hand.** Mentions don't survive copy-paste. Retype
   `@Naveen Tiles` and `@RNS International School` (and `@RNS Group` if you add
   it) in the LinkedIn editor and pick the pages from the dropdown.
3. **Check two lines are true for you.** "I'm a marketer, not a motion
   designer" and "The footage is real." I wrote them from your profile and from
   the frames: a drone shot of the actual campus and a real factory floor. If
   any clip was AI-generated, delete the footage paragraph and the "split" line
   after it.

## Why it's built this way

| Rule from the skill | How the post meets it |
|---|---|
| Hook in the first 140 chars (mobile "see more") | Line 1 is the number plus the claim. Line 2 adds the stakes |
| Number first, never open with a question | Opens on "51 seconds". The only question is the closing one |
| One contrast and one list of three, at most | Contrast: "marketer, not a motion designer". List of three: explorer, innovator, performer |
| Real stakes, not just insight | A school's admissions campaign, and a clear line on AI-generated people |
| 1,000+ chars, 20+ sentences | 1,506 chars, 21 sentences |
| No link in the body, 0-2 hashtags | Links in the first comment. Two niche hashtags |
| Em dashes and AI vocabulary | 0 em dashes. None of the flagged words |
| Native vertical video, 30-90 s | One 51.5 s 9:16 file. The reels' on-screen text works without sound |

## Timing and the first hour

- Post **Tue, Wed or Thu, 8:00-9:30 AM IST**. Your audience is in India, and
  weekend B2B posts lose 30-50% reach.
- Spend 15 minutes before posting leaving real comments on 3-5 posts in your
  feed.
- Reply to every comment in the first 90 minutes, each reply written fresh.
  Copy-paste replies get pushed down.
- Pin the post to Featured within 48 hours (see `01-profile-audit.md`).
