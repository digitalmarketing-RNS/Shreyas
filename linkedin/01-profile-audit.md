## 6. Custom URL: keep it

The skill's default is `linkedin.com/in/firstnamelastname`. **Don't change yours.**

- Your SEO work points to this exact URL: directory listings, the "Top 10
  digital marketers" LinkedIn articles and the backlinks campaign in this repo.
- LinkedIn doesn't redirect an old custom URL to a new one (per people who have
  changed theirs; LinkedIn's help page doesn't promise a redirect). Every one of
  those links would break, and someone else could claim the old URL.

I've added this exception to the skill (`experience-skills-rules.md`) so it
won't tell you to change it next time.
