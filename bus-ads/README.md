Planned 2026-10-24. Interactive version with a route map: `naveen-bus-routes.html`
(published privately at https://claude.ai/artifact/8LD5qNNtsKVPQk4baGUE7j).

**Answer:** brand one bus on each of these 10 BMTC routes. Every one runs past
one of the four Bengaluru showrooms and through areas full of home-builders and
renovators. Together the 10 buses pass within 400 m of a showroom about 110
times a day (estimate). Buy by depot, lock the route numbers in the contract,
and spot-check the buses in the Namma BMTC app.

Why each one:

1. **401-M**: busiest route past Yeshwanthpur. West Outer Ring Road through Kengeri, Nagarbhavi and Laggere, a large belt of new and rebuilt independent houses.
2. **215-H**: stops at South End Circle, 50 m from the showroom. Covers Jayanagar and JP Nagar homeowners and the new builds of JP Nagar 7th–9th Phase and Kothnur.
3. **258-CC**: the only route that passes two showrooms on every trip, and it stops right outside Rajajinagar. Then runs the Tumkur Road growth corridor to Nelamangala. Few buses run it, so the vendor must lock the bus to it. Fallback: 258-C.
4. **60-A**: old-Bengaluru homeowners (Vijayanagar, Chamarajpet, Basavanagudi, Jayanagar), the renovation market. About 15 passes a day per bus.
5. **410-FA**: second-busiest route in the set. Links Yeshwanthpur with the south-west and ends at Banashankari, 2 km from the Jayanagar showroom.
6. **80-A**: short loop through the heart of Rajajinagar, one block from the showroom. About 28 passes a day per bus, the most of any pick.
7. **365-J**: main line into Jigani along Bannerghatta Road (busiest stops of any pick). Ends 1.5 km from the showroom, so put directions on the ad.
8. **MKT-5H**: starts at KR Market (masons and contractors), passes Jayanagar, runs the Kanakapura Road new-housing corridor.
9. **176**: the most frequent route through Rajajinagar. Crosses Vijayanagar, Malleswaram and RT Nagar.
10. **398-CH**: passes the Jigani showroom door on every trip, through Anekal villages where new houses are going up. Light traffic, so it's here for the door pass, not for reach.

**Reserves:** 258-C (fallback for 258-CC), 253-HE (Hesaraghatta Rd), 501-C (Hebbal ↔ Kengeri, ORR),
215-K (Anjanapura), 369 (Jayanagar ↔ Bannerghatta Rd, depot D34), 365-N (Bannerghatta Rd past the Jigani door, only 8 trips/day).

## Depots to ask for

Agencies sell BMTC buses by depot/zone, and buses can be moved between routes
(source: bmtcbusbranding.com). Ask for: **Kothnur D34** (215-H, plus 369 and the
366-series to Jigani), **Jigani D27** (365-J, 398-CH), **Kottigepalya D31** (80-A, 401-M),
**Kengeri D12** (401-M), **Dasanapura D40** (258 family). Depot numbers come from route-variant
tags in the BMTC app data. Confidence: medium. Confirm with the vendor.

## Buying steps

1. Get quotes from three agencies with this route + depot list (full wrap and rear panel, non-AC).
2. Name each bus's route schedule in the contract, with a make-good clause if it's moved or off the road.
3. Get registration numbers; spot-check weekly in Namma BMTC live tracking; ask for monthly route proof.
4. One design per showroom area, Kannada first (required), with the landmark the bus passes:
   "Goraguntepalya Metro" (401-M, 410-FA), "South End Circle" (215-H, 60-A), "Navarang, Dr. Rajkumar Road" (258-CC, 80-A).
5. A separate WhatsApp number or QR code per showroom area, plus asking walk-ins where they saw Naveen. Compare 8 weeks before and after.
6. Run 3 months, drop the bottom two routes by leads, and swap in reserves.

Example with **assumed** figures: a ₹40,000 wrap and a ₹1.5 lakh house order at 20% margin needs about 1.3 extra orders a month.

## Method

- Data: unofficial BMTC GTFS (github.com/Vonter/bmtc-gtfs), taken from the Namma BMTC app, refreshed September 2026.
  It covers only routes with live tracking, and its timetables are approximate. Trip counts are scheduled, not ridership.
- A route "passes" a showroom when its path comes within 400 m. 1,009 route-directions qualified.
- Ranking weighs four things: closeness of the pass, frequency, how busy the route's stops are, and whether its areas are full of home-builders and renovators.
- Passes per bus per day assumes about 200 km per bus per day.
- The Rajajinagar showroom is on Dr. Rajkumar Road, which few BMTC routes use. Most Rajajinagar routes (80-A, 176) run one block away, about 250 m.
