    Book by depot, not by route

    Agencies sell BMTC buses by depot, and a bus can be moved between routes. Ask for buses from these depots, then name the route schedules in the contract.

    How to buy so the ROI holds

    1. Get quotes from three agenciesGive each agency this list of 10 routes and 5 depots. Ask for full-wrap and rear-panel prices on non-AC buses.
    2. Put route numbers in the contractName the route schedule for each bus, with a make-good clause if a bus is moved off its route or is off the road for more than a few days.
    3. Get each bus's registration numberSpot-check them weekly in the Namma BMTC app's live tracking, and ask the vendor for monthly route proof.
    4. Make one design per showroom areaKannada first (BMTC requires it), with the nearest showroom and a landmark the bus passes. Examples: "Goraguntepalya Metro" on 401-M and 410-FA, "South End Circle" on 215-H and 60-A, "Navarang, Dr. Rajkumar Road" on 258-CC and 80-A.
    5. Give each area its own numberUse a separate WhatsApp number or QR code per showroom area, and ask walk-ins where they saw Naveen. Compare 8 weeks before and after.
    6. Run 3 months, then cut the weakestDrop the bottom two routes by leads and move those buses to the reserves below.

    Formats and cost: what is confirmed

    • Full-body wraps are available. BMTC has wrapped 3,400 AC and non-AC buses since 2025. Every ad needs BMTC approval and must be in Kannada, and BMTC can keep up to 10% of the space for public notices.The Hindu, 17 Jul 2026
    • The 1,000 Tata non-AC electric buses take rear-panel ads only, an 18 sq ft panel, under a 3-year rights tender. If a depot gives you these buses, you get the rear panel and no wrap.Times of India, 9 Jul 2026
    • BMTC's floor price for those rear panels is ₹4,800 per bus per month plus GST. That is what the agency pays BMTC. Your price will be higher.The Hindu, 17 Jul 2026
    • Minimum order is usually 5 bus-months. One agency claims about 50,000 daily views for a full non-AC wrap and 22,000 for a rear panel. Treat those as sales claims.bmtcbusbranding.com, agency site
    Break-even check per bus orders needed per month = monthly cost per bus ÷ gross margin per order Example with assumed figures: a ₹40,000 wrap and a ₹1.5 lakh house order at 20% margin (₹30,000) needs about 1.3 extra orders a month per bus. Plug in your real numbers.

    If budget is tight, wrap #1–#5 and put rear panels on #6–#10. Rear panels still get long looks in Bengaluru traffic.
