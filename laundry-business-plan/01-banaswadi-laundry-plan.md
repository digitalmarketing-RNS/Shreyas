# Laundry Business Plan: Dodda Banaswadi Main Road, Bengaluru 560033

Prepared 2 October 2026. Location from the shared map pin: next to Srushti PG for Ladies, Dodda Banaswadi Main Rd, near the fire station, Ram Gopal Layout, Banaswadi. Shop reviewed from the 21-second store video.

---

## 1. Verdict

**Yes, it can work, but only as a pickup-and-delivery laundry with ironing and PG subscriptions.** It will not work as a shop that waits for walk-ins. The street in the video is residential, so walk-in traffic will be low. Your customers are the PGs and homes within 1.5–2 km.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Real cash needed | **About ₹12.3 lakh**: ₹2L refundable deposit, ₹7.8L setup and ₹2.5L running buffer. Your list adds up to ₹9.6L and has no salaries. |
| Monthly break-even | **About ₹88–90k revenue a month**, roughly 30 kg of washing plus 60 ironing pieces a day (about 110–130 regular customers) |
| Base case | Monthly profit from month 3. **About ₹50k profit a month from month 10.** The ₹7.8L setup cost comes back in about 22 months. |
| ROI | About **55–60% a year from year 2** on the money you can't get back (₹10.3L). About 50% if you count the deposit. |
| Main condition | You run the counter and sales yourself for the first 6 months. A paid manager costs ₹20–25k a month and pushes payback past 3 years. |

Many residents are working professionals who commute to Manyata and the ORR, and the older independent houses nearby create steady ironing demand.
- Ground floor with a rolling shutter. The floor is newly tiled and the walls are painted, so you need less interior work.
- There is a main room plus a back area with a green door and a grilled window. Put the washer and dryer at the back, near the drain, and vent the dryer through the window. Put the counter and ironing table at the front, near the shutter, where people on the road can see the steam ironing.

- [ ] Water source (BWSSB, borewell or sump) and storage for summer shortages.

## 7. Capacity

| Equipment | Realistic daily capacity |
|---|---|
| 10–12 kg commercial washer (45–60 min cycle, ~8 kg per load, 8–10 loads in 12 hours) | 65–80 kg/day |
| Dryer, same size or bigger (usually the bottleneck, so buy it at least as big as the washer) | 65–80 kg/day |
| 1 ironer with vacuum table + steam iron (25–30 pcs/hr) | 150–200 pcs/day |

- Give each customer a **numbered mesh bag**. Then you can mix several customers' clothes by colour and fabric into full machine loads without losing any item. Washing one customer per load cuts your capacity in half; sell that as the premium private wash instead.
- When you pass **50 kg/day**, buy a second washer: ₹45–60k for a heavy-duty domestic model or ₹1.1–1.3L for a commercial one.

## 12. Operations

**Order flow**
1. Pickup in two slots: 8–11 am and 6–9 pm, which suit PG timings.
2. Intake: weigh, count pieces, note stains and damage, take a photo, tag the items, bag them in a numbered mesh bag, and send the bill on WhatsApp.
3. Sort by colour and fabric, and pre-treat stains.
4. Wash, then dry.
5. Fold or iron, then do a quality check.
6. Pack with the bill.
7. Deliver within 24–48 hours, collect payment by UPI, and ask for a Google review.

**Hours:** 8 am–9 pm, 7 days a week. Rotate staff weekly offs.

**Shop layout**
- **Rear:** washer-dryer stack near the drain, dryer vented through the window, sorting bins, chemical shelf.
- **Middle:** folding table and racks for ready orders, labelled by order number.
- **Front:** counter, weighing scale, and the ironing table near the shutter, where the road can see it.

**Software:** a laundry POS app (₹500–1,500 a month), or start with a Google Sheet and a WhatsApp Business catalogue. It must handle order IDs, tags, "ready" alerts and customer history.

**Washing rules**
- Never mix whites and colours.
- Check pockets.
- Wash innerwear and towels separately, with sanitiser.
- Put delicates in mesh bags.
- No high heat for wool or synthetics.
- Test new garments for colour bleed.

**Water:** keep 2,000–3,000 L of storage and a tanker contact for summer, and install the softener.

## 13. Marketing: first 90 days

**Two weeks before opening**
- Set up a Google Business Profile with the categories "Laundry service" and "Dry cleaner", plus real photos. Get the first 20 reviews from real orders placed by friends and family.
- Get a WhatsApp Business number with a catalogue and price list, and put the QR code on every bag, bill and flyer.
- Visit 30–40 PGs within 2 km. Offer residents a free first wash up to 3 kg. Offer owners a linen contract, and give the warden or owner 10% on referred business.

**Launch month**
- Distribute 2,000 flyers to PGs and apartments, using notice boards where permitted (₹5–8k).
- Post Instagram and Facebook reels of the steam press, and run 2 km radius ads at ₹200–300 a day for 30 days.
- Run a referral offer: both people get 2 kg free.
- Launch prices: ₹69/kg, first pickup free, ironing ₹10/pc.

**Ongoing**
- Give 50% off every 10th order and push the prepaid plans.
- Ask for a Google review at every delivery.
- Set up apartment tie-ups with a fixed weekly pickup day per complex.
- Sign 5 salons, clinics or gyms for linen by month 3.
- Add free listings on Justdial and Sulekha.

## 14. Compliance checklist

- [ ] Rent agreement with a commercial-use clause, lock-in, escalation ≤ 5%, stamped and registered.
- [ ] BBMP trade licence (laundry or dry-cleaning category).
- [ ] Karnataka Shops & Commercial Establishments registration (within 30 days of opening).
- [ ] Udyam (MSME) registration (free).
- [ ] BESCOM commercial connection and load enhancement to about 12–15 kW.
- [ ] Current account and a UPI QR in the business name.
- [ ] **GST:** not mandatory below ₹20L turnover. If you cross it, compare the composition scheme for service providers (6%, turnover up to ₹50L) with regular GST (laundry is 18%). Ask a CA.
- [ ] KSPCB: a small laundry without a boiler is usually low-category. Confirm with a CA or consultant, and don't install a large steam boiler.
- [ ] Professional tax enrolment once you have employees. Check with a CA.
- [ ] Insurance covering the shop, equipment and customer goods.
- [ ] ABC fire extinguisher and a first-aid kit.

## 15. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Garment damage or loss claims | Intake photos, tagging, CCTV, damage policy printed on the bill, insurance |
| Single washer breaks down | Service contract (AMC) with a vendor offering 24–48 hr service, backup arrangement with a nearby laundry, second washer at 50 kg/day |
| Summer water shortage | Storage tank, tanker contract, softener |
| Power cuts and voltage swings | Stabiliser, schedule dryer runs |
| Staff leave or steal | Train two people, per-piece incentives, CCTV, police verification |
| App price war (₹69/kg players) | Compete on speed, trust and subscriptions, not on price |
| PGs have their own washing machines | Sell ironing, bedsheet contracts, and wash & iron for professionals |
| Owner is absent | The biggest risk. Be at the shop full-time for the first 6 months. |
| Landlord objects to water use or noise | Get written permission in the agreement |

## 16. Is another business a better fit for this shop?

| Business | Investment | Margin | Fit for this shop | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Laundry + ironing + dry-clean pickup point + alterations** | ₹10–12L | 50–65% gross | High: PG belt, repeat customers, no stock, quick commerce can't replace it | **Best fit** |
| Laundry franchise (Tumbledry or UClean) | ₹18–25L | Royalty about 7–7.5% of sales | Both already have stores in Ramamurthy Nagar | Lower ROI for you |
| Tiffin or cloud kitchen for PG residents | ₹6–10L | 25–35% | Medium: most PGs include food, plus FSSAI, wastage and gas | Only with cooking experience |
| Kirana or grocery | ₹6–10L (mostly stock) | 8–15% | Low: Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart are strong here | Avoid |
| Tailoring and alterations | ₹1–2L | 60%+ | High **as an add-on** | Add it inside the laundry |
| Tea, snacks or juice | ₹4–6L | 50–60% | Low: residential lane with little footfall | Avoid |
| Mobile repair or accessories | ₹3–5L | 20–40% | Low: depends on skill, heavy competition | Avoid |
| Xerox, print or courier | ₹2–4L | 20–30% | Low ticket size | Courier drop point only, as an add-on |

**Best-value combination:** laundry, steam ironing and dry-clean collection (outsourced), plus an **alteration tailor on commission** sitting in your shop (you keep 30–40%) and a **courier drop point**. The add-ons bring in an extra ₹10–25k a month with almost no extra investment and increase footfall.
