Smallest space this works in

Back room: about 7 ft wide, with a right wall at least 6½ ft long. Front shop: about 8 ft wide and 10 ft deep.

Why it's laid out this way

  • No toilet neededThe old counter in the back room already has water pipes and a sink drain at the window wall. The sink, both washers and the floor trap all connect there.
  • Machines replace the old counterThe right wall is about 7 ft long and the two stacks need 4¾ ft of it. Stacking is what makes three machines fit in a 7½ ft room.
  • Folding happens under the windowDaylight makes stains easy to spot, and the folding counter is one step from the dryer.
  • The counter sits on the shutter lineCustomers are served at the front, the way small shops in the area work, so the 9 ft shop needs no waiting area. A stool covers anyone who steps in.
  • Ready orders are stored up the wallFloor-to-7-ft pigeonholes on the left wall hold 60–80 packed orders in 1½ ft of depth. The 2-tier rail doubles the hanging space for shirts.
  • Dirty bags stay on one sideBags go from the counter straight up the left side into the bins. They never go on the folding counter, the ironing table or the pigeonholes.

Plumber

  • SupplyOld water point → sediment filter → softener → 20 mm line behind the stacks at 1.1 m. One valve per washer and one for the sink.
  • PressureCheck the minimum water pressure in each washer's manual. Add a small booster pump if they fill slowly.
  • DrainEach washer pumps into a 50 mm standpipe. The standpipes, the sink and the floor trap all join the old sink drain at the window wall.
  • FloorReplace the old 1 ft tiles with anti-skid tiles, sloped about 1 in 100 towards the floor trap, with 150 mm tiled skirting. The front shop's tiles stay as they are.
  • SinkSmall steel utility sink, about 450 × 400 mm, beside the folding counter. Add a splash guard on the folding-counter side.
  • Vent + fan100 mm aluminium dryer duct and a 300 mm exhaust fan, both through the window. Clean the dryer's lint filter after every load.

Check these before work starts

  • Count the tiles in both rooms (2 ft in front, 1 ft at the back) and measure the window's sill and top.
  • Measure the back room's right wall, where the black counter stands. The stacks need about 6¼ ft of it, counting the 1½ ft gap from the window wall. If the wall is shorter, move the softener elsewhere and start Stack 1 nearer the window wall.
  • Measure the white door's clear width. Commercial machines are about 700 mm wide. If the opening is under about 750 mm, take the door off its hinges to bring them in.
  • Confirm that the old water point and the sink drain at the window wall both work.
  • Get the landlord's written permission to remove the black counter, re-tile the back room, fit a vent plate in the window and install the exhaust fan.
  • Apply to BESCOM for a 3-phase connection before you order the machines.
