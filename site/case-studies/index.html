Naveen Tiles

Manufacturing · Karnataka Semrush + live SERP

From no commercial visibility at all to #1 nationally for three core manufacturing keywords — and holding those positions for more than two years through multiple core updates.

Starting point

Zero Google visibility on every commercial keyword

Keywords at #1

Top tiles company · Tile company in India · Tile manufacturer

Held for

2+ years, through several core updates

Scope

National organic, not local

Verification

Semrush position history plus a live search you can run now

Channel mix

Organic only — no paid support

Naveen Tiles is a manufacturer, which is a specific kind of SEO problem. The buyers are distributors, builders and architects, the search volumes are modest, and the queries are dominated by aggregators — IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Justdial — which occupy the first page of almost every industrial term in India.

The company had a website that described itself accurately and ranked for nothing commercial. A manufacturer's natural instinct is to write about the factory; the searcher is trying to work out whether this supplier can fulfil their order. Those are different pages.

The work was mostly structural. Building genuine product and capability pages targeting the terms buyers actually use, fixing an architecture that buried everything commercial several clicks deep, and establishing authority through industry sources rather than generic directories — which is what puts a manufacturer above an aggregator rather than alongside it.

Rankings arrived over months rather than weeks, which is normal for a domain with no commercial history. What matters more is what happened afterwards: they stayed. Two-plus years at #1 across several core updates means the position rests on the site genuinely being the better answer, not on a tactic with a shelf life.

What this one shows.

Slow-built rankings are durable rankings. Everything here was structural — architecture, genuine depth, relevant authority. Nothing was done that a core update could take away, which is exactly why no core update has.

RNSIT

Higher education · Bangalore Google Search Console

A 1000% increase in organic sessions across the admissions window, with #1 positions on the highest-intent education keywords in Bangalore — retained across multiple admission cycles.

+1000% across the admissions window

Keywords at #1

Best engineering college in Bangalore · Top MCA college in Bangalore · Core branches of engineering

Retained

Across multiple admission cycles

Difficulty

One of the most contested local SERPs in Indian education

Verification

Search Console data, screenshots on request

Competition

Colleges with substantially larger marketing budgets

Education SEO in Bangalore is unusually brutal. The intent is seasonal and concentrated — admissions decisions happen in a window of a few months, after which the traffic is worth a fraction of what it was. The competition includes institutions with dedicated marketing departments. And the queries are superlatives: "best", "top", "top 10", which Google treats with particular suspicion because every institution claims them.

The insight that changed the strategy was that "best engineering college in Bangalore" is not really a commercial query at the moment it's typed. It's a research query from a seventeen-year-old and their parents, and the pages that win it are the ones that genuinely help with the decision — placement data, fee structure, cut-offs, branch comparisons, hostel details — rather than the ones that shout loudest about being the best.

So we built for the actual question. "Core branches of engineering" is a good example: a genuinely informational term, targeted deliberately, which reaches students earlier in the decision and brings them into the site before they've formed a shortlist. That page ranks #1 and does work that no amount of superlative-chasing would.

The 1000% session increase is the headline, but the durable result is that the rankings returned in the next admissions cycle and the one after. Seasonal SEO that has to be rebuilt every year isn't an asset; this is.

What this one shows.

Winning a superlative keyword usually means being the most genuinely useful page rather than the most assertive one. And reaching searchers earlier — informational terms adjacent to the money keyword — frequently beats competing head-on for the money keyword alone.

Registration Arena

Business services · Pune GSC + Google Business Profile Insights

20+ qualified leads a month from organic search alone, with top positions in both the organic results and the Google Maps pack for the primary commercial keyword.

Qualified leads

20+ per month from organic alone

Primary keyword

Company registration in Pune — top positions, organic and Maps

Channel

Organic + map pack together

Ad spend

Zero

Verification

Search Console and GBP Insights, available on request

Lead quality

Enquiries from businesses actually ready to register

Registration Arena handles company incorporation and compliance in Pune. It's a high-intent local service: someone searching "company registration in Pune" is ready to hire, usually this week, and the enquiry is worth a great deal more than a click on a blog post.

It's also a category Google treats carefully. Corporate compliance touches people's money and legal standing, which puts it near YMYL territory — expertise and trust signals carry more weight than they would for a restaurant, and thin pages get nowhere.

Two things had to work at once. The organic result needed real depth on process, documents, timelines and cost, because that's what the searcher is actually trying to find out and it demonstrates the expertise Google is looking for. And the Google Business Profile needed to be strong enough to hold a map-pack slot, because for a local professional service the map pack captures a large share of the clicks before anyone reaches the organic results.

Working both together is what produced the lead volume. Ranking organically while absent from the map pack leaves most of the traffic to three competitors sitting above you. The two channels compound; running only one leaves most of the available enquiries on the table.

What this one shows.

For a local service business, organic and the map pack are one job, not two. And in a near-YMYL category, the depth that satisfies Google's quality expectations is the same depth that convinces a nervous buyer — the SEO work and the sales work point the same direction.

Methodology

How to read any SEO case study, including these

Case studies are the easiest thing in this industry to fake, so here is what to interrogate — applied to mine as much as anyone else's.

  • Is the client named? An anonymised case study cannot be checked. That's usually the point of anonymising it.
  • Is the keyword named? "Ranked #1" is meaningless without the term. #1 for a phrase nobody searches is trivially easy.
  • Is the baseline stated? "300% traffic growth" from a base of 40 visits is 120 visits. Percentages without a starting number are decoration.
  • Where did the number come from? Search Console and GBP Insights are first-party. Third-party tool estimates are estimates — useful for direction, not evidence.
  • How long did it hold? A screenshot captures one moment. Two years through multiple core updates is a claim about the method, not about luck.
  • Can you talk to the client? This is the one that settles it. If a consultant won't connect you, ask yourself why.

Every result on this page satisfies all six. Ask me for the screenshots, or ask me to connect you with the client — both offers are real, and you should make the same demand of everyone else you're considering.
