At ₹4,000 a month, a vendor can afford roughly four to six hours of anyone's attention. Real SEO doesn't fit in that, so what gets delivered instead is an automated audit PDF and a batch of purchased links. Those links are exactly what Google's link spam systems are built to neutralise, and cleaning up afterwards costs more than doing it properly would have. My ₹4,000 plan is deliberately narrow — Google Business Profile only — because that scope genuinely fits the budget. Anyone offering full SEO at that price is offering something else.

Variables

What actually changes the number

Any honest quote moves with these six things. If someone quotes you without asking about them, they're quoting a package rather than your situation.

How contested your keywords are

"Dentist in Jayanagar" and "best engineering college in Bangalore" are not the same job. The second needs several times the content and authority work, so it costs more and takes longer.

The state of your site

A well-built site on a modern CMS is cheap to work on. A ten-year-old site with a broken URL structure needs foundational work before optimisation makes any sense.

How many pages actually matter

Optimising eight commercial pages is a different engagement from a 4,000-SKU catalogue where the architecture itself is the problem.

Whether you have history

An established domain with existing rankings moves faster and cheaper than a new domain starting from zero. Age isn't a ranking factor, but accumulated authority is.

Local, national or both

Local SEO for one location is contained. National rankings against well-funded competitors need sustained content and link investment.

Who ships the changes

If I can implement directly, work moves fast. If every change queues behind a development team with a three-week sprint, the same work takes months — and I'd rather price that honestly.

Questions

Questions about cost

What is the average cost of SEO in Bangalore?

For a competent freelancer, ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 per month. For an agency, ₹30,000 to ₹75,000+ for comparable scope. Project work such as a one-off technical audit typically runs ₹15,000 to ₹40,000 depending on site size. Anything advertised below about ₹5,000 for full SEO is almost always automated reporting and purchased links.

How much does a freelance SEO consultant charge per hour?

Experienced freelance SEOs in Bangalore charge roughly ₹500 to ₹1,500 per hour. I generally prefer monthly retainers to hourly billing, because SEO results come from sustained work rather than counted hours, and hourly billing pushes both sides toward measuring the wrong thing.

Why is SEO charged monthly rather than as a one-off?

Because competitors keep working, Google keeps updating, and rankings you stop maintaining decay. A one-off audit is genuinely useful and I'll do one. But the businesses that hold #1 for years do so because someone keeps at it — my longest-standing rankings have survived multiple core updates precisely because nobody stopped.

Do you offer a discount for a longer commitment?

No, and I'd be wary of anyone who does. A discount for a twelve-month commitment is buying your inability to leave. My plans are month to month at the same price, and the average client stays over two years anyway.

What is not included in these prices?

Paid tools licensed in your own name, paid directory listings where you choose to buy them, content in languages other than English, and development work beyond SEO-related fixes. I'll flag any of these before they arise rather than adding them to an invoice. There are no setup fees and no cancellation fees.

Is cheap SEO ever worth it?

Below roughly ₹5,000 a month for full SEO, the economics don't work — nobody can do meaningful work in the hours that budget buys, so the deliverable becomes an automated report and a batch of purchased links. Those links are what Google's link spam systems target, and cleanup afterwards costs more than doing it properly would have.

Can I start small and scale up?

Yes, and for most local businesses that's the sensible order. Start at ₹4,000 with Google Business Profile work, because the map pack usually produces the fastest return for a single-location business. Move up once it's paying for itself.

What happens if it doesn't work?

You stop paying, with no notice period and no exit fee. You keep every account, every dashboard and all the historical data, because they were always in your name. I'd also tell you before you did — if three months in the data says I can't move your numbers, you'll hear it from me first.
