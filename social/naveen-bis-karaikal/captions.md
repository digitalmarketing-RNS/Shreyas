- BIS licence **CM/L-6700069315**, brand **NAVEEN DIAMONTILE**, scale Large
- Granted 20/12/2018, valid till 19/12/2029

The copy says "new varieties are included" and "all these varieties are BIS certified".
It does not say "5 new varieties", because the licence list may include varieties that were certified before this update.

## LinkedIn

We're pleased to share an update from our Karaikal plant in Puducherry.

The Bureau of Indian Standards has approved our application for product inclusion. Every variety we applied for is now included in our BIS certification under IS 15622:2017, and the plant is cleared to begin manufacturing them.

Now covered under BIS Licence CM/L-6700069315 (brand: Naveen Diamontile):

• 600 × 600 mm polished glazed floor tile, 9.5 mm (Group BIb)
• 400 × 400 mm glazed floor tile, 11 mm (Group BIIa)
• 450 × 300 mm glazed wall tile, 7.5 mm (Group BIII)
• 300 × 300 mm glazed floor & wall tile, 7.5 mm (Group BIII)
• Unglazed vitrified tiles in all sizes, polished, rectified and unrectified (Group BIa)

For architects, builders and dealers, this means you can specify these tiles with independent, BIS-verified quality behind them.

Thank you to our team at Karaikal, and to our partners and customers for their trust and support.
